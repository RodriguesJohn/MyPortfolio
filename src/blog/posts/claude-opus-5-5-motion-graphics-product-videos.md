## What's new in Claude Opus 5.5?

These are the improvements that matter most for product content:

- **It's 20% cheaper than Opus 5.** The top model now costs less.
- **Cheaper options are available.** Sonnet 5.5 is 50% cheaper and Haiku 4.5 is 75% cheaper, though both are less capable.
- **Improved support for video and motion graphics**, like the promo above.

## FAQ

### What is Claude Opus 5.5 good at?

Claude Opus 5.5 is Anthropic's most capable model in the Claude 5 family. One of its standout improvements is support for video and motion graphics work, such as product promo videos, launch videos, and UI animations.

### How does Claude Opus 5.5 edit videos?

It connects to editing and creative tools through MCP (Model Context Protocol). With the right integrations set up, it can help with both the editing and the creative process of a video.

### How much cheaper is Claude Opus 5.5 than Opus 5?

Opus 5.5 is 20% cheaper than Opus 5. For lower cost, Sonnet 5.5 is 50% cheaper and Haiku 4.5 is 75% cheaper, though both are less capable.

### Can Claude make a product launch video?

Yes. Human AI Studio used Claude Opus 5.5 to make a promo video for Orbi AI, a voice-to-content app, and a Dynamic Island UI animation. Both are in this article.
